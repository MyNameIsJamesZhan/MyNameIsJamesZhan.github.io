## First Impressions

Out of the box, the Q1 Pro feels premium. The aluminum case is dense and solid — no flex, no rattle. The gasket mount gives it a noticeably softer keypress feel compared to tray-mount boards, which I appreciated after long typing sessions.

## Build Quality

Excellent. The anodized aluminum finish is clean, the RGB diffusion through the south-facing LEDs is even, and the USB-C port is nicely recessed. The included coiled cable is a nice touch.

## Typing Experience

I went with the Gateron G Pro 3.0 Red switches (linear). Out of the box they're slightly scratchy but lube up nicely with a basic application of Krytox 205g0. The stock foam dampening eliminates most of the hollow sound.

The gasket mount definitely contributes to a bouncier, more comfortable typing experience for extended sessions. Less finger fatigue compared to my previous budget board.

## Wireless Performance

2.4GHz connection via the included dongle is rock solid — no perceptible latency for coding or general use. Bluetooth mode is fine for casual use but I wouldn't recommend it for gaming. Battery life is solid; I get about 2 weeks on a charge with RGB at ~30%.

## Software

Keychron's VIA support is solid. Remapping keys and configuring layers is straightforward through the VIA web app. No bloatware required.

## Criticism

- South-facing LEDs mean legends don't shine through as brightly on some keycap sets.
- The function row could be slightly larger — it's cramped on the 75% layout.
- Price is on the higher end; the wired Q1 v2 is cheaper if you don't need wireless.

## Verdict

A well-rounded 75% board for someone who wants wireless flexibility without compromising on build quality or customization. Worth the price if you spend serious time typing.
